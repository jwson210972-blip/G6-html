About Spongebob

Spongbob is a character in spongbob squarepants. Also an energetic, funny character. Spongebob got famous because his cartoon was very funny, easy to watch for both kids and adults. Also,Stephen Hillenburg created SpongeBob SquarePants. The series created in 1999. Spongebob faces simple but funny problems every day, like trying to pass his boating test to get his driver's license. He also has to protect the secret Krabby Patty formula from Plankton, who always tries to steal it. Even when he faces scary monsters or mean neighbors like Squidward, SpongeBob uses his happy attitude and cooking skills to save the day.

Facts

  • SpongeBob's first name was going to be SpongeBoy. The creators changed it because a mop company already owned the trademark.
  • Creator Stephen Hillberg studied marine biology and taught it before he started making cartoons.
  • Squidward is an octopus, not a squid. He only has six tentacles to make animation easier
  • Mr. Krabs's restaurant is shaped like a wooden lobster or crab trap.
